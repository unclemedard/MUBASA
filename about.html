Who We Are

The Makerere University Bafumbira Students Association (MUBASA) is a registered students' organization established in 2023 at Makerere University Business School (MUBS) in Nakawa, Kampala. We represent students of Bafumbira descent studying at Uganda's premier business institution, creating a home away from home for individuals who share a common heritage, language, and cultural identity.

The Bafumbira people are an ethnic group native to Kisoro District in South Western Uganda, residing primarily in the beautiful highlands near the borders of Rwanda and the Democratic Republic of Congo. Known for their rich cultural traditions, agricultural practices, and strong community bonds, the Bafumbira have a history that stretches back centuries, shaped by the volcanic landscapes of the Virunga Mountains and the interconnectedness of the Great Lakes region.

At MUBASA, we believe that maintaining our cultural identity while pursuing higher education is not only possible but essential. Our association provides a platform where students can speak their language, practice their traditions, and support each other through the challenges of university life. We are proud of our heritage and committed to ensuring that future generations of Bafumbira students continue to celebrate who they are.

Registered students' association at MUBS since 2023

Governed by a democratically elected executive committee
Supported by a council of elders for cultural guidance

Our Journey

Key milestones in the history of MUBASA

2023

Foundation of MUBASA

A group of passionate Bafumbira students came together to form MUBASA, establishing the first formal organization for Bafumbira students at MUBS with the vision of unity and cultural preservation.

2023

Elders Council Established

Recognizing the need for cultural guidance, MUBASA formally established an Elders Council comprising respected community members to advise the association on cultural matters and mentor young members.

2024

First Cultural Day

MUBASA hosted its inaugural Cultural Day event, bringing together students, staff, and community members to celebrate Bafumbira traditions, dance, and cuisine at the MUBS campus.

2024

First Community Outreach

MUBASA members traveled to Kisoro District for the first community outreach program, visiting schools, donating books, and engaging with secondary school students to inspire academic excellence.

2024

Award of Excellence

MUBASA was recognized by the MUBS Guild Union as the Most Active Cultural Association, acknowledging the organization's contributions to campus diversity and student engagement.

2024

Expanded Programs

MUBASA launched new initiatives including a language preservation program, an alumni mentorship network, and increased community outreach projects in Kisoro District.

Our Purpose

The foundation that guides every MUBASA initiative

Our Mission

To unite all Bafumbira students at Makerere University Business School in a spirit of solidarity and mutual support, fostering academic excellence through peer-to-peer learning and mentorship programs. We are dedicated to preserving and promoting the rich cultural heritage of the Bafumbira people, ensuring that the Kifumbira language continues to thrive among the younger generation.

We strive to develop transformative leaders who will positively impact their communities, champion sustainable development in Kisoro District, and contribute to Uganda's growth while maintaining strong connections to their roots.

Our Vision

To be the most united, culturally vibrant, and academically excelling students' association at Makerere University Business School, setting a benchmark for other ethnic student organizations across Uganda's higher education institutions.

We envision a future where every Bafumbira student at MUBS feels a strong sense of belonging, where our cultural traditions are celebrated and passed down to future generations, and where MUBASA alumni continue to make meaningful contributions to society as ethical leaders, innovative entrepreneurs, and community champions.

Our Core Values

The principles that define who we are and how we operate

Unity

We believe in the power of togetherness. MUBASA exists because Bafumbira students chose to unite rather than remain isolated. We support one another through academic challenges, celebrate together during cultural events, and stand by each other in times of need. Our unity is our greatest strength.

Integrity

We uphold the highest standards of honesty, transparency, and accountability in all our dealings. Our leaders are entrusted with resources and responsibilities, and we take this trust seriously. We believe that integrity is the foundation of good leadership and lasting community trust.

Innovation

While we honor our traditions, we also embrace new ideas and approaches. We encourage creative thinking in solving community problems, adopting modern tools for cultural preservation, and finding innovative ways to support our members' academic and professional growth.

Inclusivity

MUBASA is open to all Bafumbira students regardless of their background, gender, religion, or political affiliation. We celebrate diversity within our community and believe that every member has something valuable to contribute. Discrimination has no place in our association.

Excellence

We strive for excellence in everything we do, from academic performance to cultural programs and community service. We set high standards for ourselves and continuously work to improve. We believe that the Bafumbira community deserves nothing less than our best efforts.

Heritage Preservation

We take pride in the rich cultural heritage of the Bafumbira people and are committed to preserving it for future generations. Through language programs, traditional dance, storytelling, and cultural education, we ensure that our identity remains strong even as we embrace modernity.

Bafumbira Cultural Heritage

Understanding the rich traditions that define our people

The Kifumbira Language

Kifumbira is a Bantu language spoken by the Bafumbira people. It shares similarities with Kinyarwanda and Kisoro-area languages but has distinct characteristics that make it unique. At MUBASA, we run language preservation programs where fluent speakers teach younger members, ensuring that our language continues to thrive even among those who grew up in urban areas.

Our language is more than a means of communication; it carries our proverbs, our history, and our worldview. Through Kifumbira, we pass down wisdom from one generation to the next.

Traditional Music & Dance

Bafumbira music and dance are vibrant expressions of our culture. Traditional dances like the energetic drumming performances and celebratory dances are central to our cultural events. These performances tell stories of our history, celebrate harvests, and honor important community milestones.

MUBASA's Cultural Day features captivating performances where members showcase these traditional art forms, keeping them alive and introducing them to the broader MUBS community.

Traditional Cuisine

Bafumbira cuisine reflects our agricultural heritage, with dishes centered on millet, sorghum, beans, and vegetables grown in the fertile volcanic soils of Kisoro. Traditional preparations like ugali from millet flour, various bean dishes, and locally grown greens are not just food; they represent our connection to the land.

During MUBASA gatherings, members prepare and share traditional foods, creating a taste of home for those far from Kisoro and an educational experience for those unfamiliar with our culinary traditions.

Traditional Attire

Traditional Bafumbira clothing features beautifully woven fabrics and garments that reflect our cultural identity. The mishanana and other traditional outfits are worn during ceremonies and cultural celebrations, symbolizing pride in our heritage and respect for our ancestors.

MUBASA encourages members to wear traditional attire during major events, promoting visibility and pride in Bafumbira culture within the university community.
