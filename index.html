Who We Are

The Makerere University Bafumbira Students Association (MUBASA) is a vibrant community of students hailing from the Bafumbira ethnic group in Kisoro District, South Western Uganda. Founded on the principles of unity, cultural preservation, and academic excellence, MUBASA serves as a home away from home for Bafumbira students pursuing their studies at Makerere University Business School.

Our association is built on the belief that together we can achieve more. We provide a supportive network where members can connect, share experiences, and grow both academically and personally. Through our various programs and initiatives, we strive to maintain the rich traditions of the Bafumbira people while embracing the opportunities that higher education presents.

Our Purpose

The driving force behind everything we do at MUBASA

Our Mission

To unite all Bafumbira students at Makerere University Business School, foster academic excellence, preserve and promote Bafumbira culture and the Kifumbira language, and develop transformative leaders who will positively impact their communities and Uganda at large.

Our Vision

To be the most united, culturally vibrant, and academically excelling students' association at Makerere University Business School, producing leaders who champion development while preserving the rich heritage of the Bafumbira people for generations to come.

Programs & Activities

Engaging initiatives that bring our community together

Cultural Celebrations

Annual cultural day events featuring traditional Bafumbira dances, music performances, storytelling sessions, and cuisine exhibitions. These celebrations strengthen cultural identity and provide a platform for artistic expression among members.

Academic Mentorship

Peer-to-peer tutoring, study groups, and exam preparation workshops led by senior students. Our mentorship program pairs struggling students with high-performing mentors to ensure academic excellence across all years of study.

Language Classes

Weekly Kifumbira language sessions taught by fluent speakers and elders. These classes help younger members maintain their mother tongue while also welcoming non-Bafumbira students interested in learning about the language and culture.

Community Outreach

Regular visits to Kisoro District schools to donate books, offer mentorship, and inspire academic excellence among secondary school students. Our outreach extends to supporting vulnerable community members through welfare programs.

Sports & Recreation

Inter-faculty sports tournaments, fitness sessions, and recreational activities that promote physical health and team spirit. Our football and netball teams compete in various university leagues, bringing members together through sports.

Leadership Development

Workshops, seminars, and training programs designed to nurture leadership skills among members. We believe in developing tomorrow's leaders today through practical experiences and mentorship from successful alumni.
