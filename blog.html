Annual Cultural Day Celebrations 2024: A Resounding Success

The Makerere University Bafumbira Students Association hosted its annual Cultural Day on Saturday, January 13, 2024, at the MUBS Nakawa campus grounds. The event brought together over 300 attendees including students, staff, alumni, and guests from the Bafumbira community. The day was filled with vibrant traditional dances, captivating storytelling sessions, a showcase of Bafumbira cuisine, and a fashion display featuring traditional attire.

The keynote address was delivered by the association president, who emphasized the importance of cultural preservation among the youth. The Elders Council was also present, blessing the event and sharing wisdom with the younger generation.

MUBASA Community Outreach Makes Impact in Kisoro Schools

In a remarkable display of commitment to their home district, twenty MUBASA members traveled to Kisoro during the December holidays to conduct a community outreach program. The team visited five secondary schools distributing over 500 textbooks and stationery items to students.

Beyond material support, the MUBASA team conducted mentorship sessions where they shared their experiences of transitioning from village schools to university life. The sessions covered study skills, career guidance, and the importance of preserving cultural identity while pursuing modern education.

New Executive Committee Sworn In for 2023-2024 Academic Year

Following successful elections held in October, the new MUBASA Executive Committee was formally sworn in during a ceremony at the MUBS Conference Hall. The event was attended by members, alumni, representatives from the MUBS Guild Union, and members of the Elders Council who presided over the oath-taking ceremony.

The new committee, led by President John Bosco Byamukama, has pledged to focus on four key areas: strengthening member welfare programs, expanding cultural activities, enhancing academic support initiatives, and increasing community outreach projects.

Kifumbira Language Classes Launch to Preserve Mother Tongue

MUBASA has launched a groundbreaking Kifumbira language preservation program aimed at ensuring that young Bafumbira students maintain fluency in their mother tongue. The weekly classes, held every Saturday afternoon at the MUBS campus, are taught by fluent speakers from the Elders Council and advanced members.

The curriculum covers conversational Kifumbira, traditional proverbs and their meanings, storytelling techniques, and cultural poetry. The program has already attracted over 80 registered participants, including non-Bafumbira students curious about the language.

MUBASA Academic Excellence Program: Improving Student Performance

The MUBASA Academic Excellence Program has recorded impressive results in its first semester of operation, with participating members showing an average GPA improvement of 0.4 points. The program pairs senior students with juniors in peer-to-peer tutoring sessions covering challenging courses.

The program also includes study groups, exam preparation workshops, and access to past papers and study materials. "I was struggling with Financial Accounting until my senior mentor from MUBASA helped me understand the concepts," says Prossy Ninsiima, a second-year student.

MUBASA Alumni Network Launches Professional Mentorship Platform

The newly established MUBASA Alumni Network has launched a professional mentorship platform connecting current students with graduates working in various industries. The platform features over 30 mentors from fields including banking, entrepreneurship, public service, and international development.

"Our alumni want to give back to the community that shaped them," says the Alumni Chair. The mentorship program offers career guidance, internship connections, and professional development workshops.
