Join MUBASA

Membership is free and open to all Bafumbira students at MUBS

Why Join MUBASA?

Becoming a member of MUBASA connects you to a supportive community of Bafumbira students dedicated to academic success, cultural preservation, and mutual support. Membership is completely free and comes with numerous benefits.

Connect with fellow Bafumbira students
Access academic support and tutoring
Participate in cultural events and celebrations
Receive welfare support when needed
Network with alumni and professionals
Learn and preserve Bafumbira culture

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about MUBASA

Who can join MUBASA?

MUBASA membership is open to all students of Bafumbira origin currently enrolled at Makerere University Business School (MUBS). Membership is free, and all you need is a valid student ID and willingness to abide by the association's constitution. Students from other institutions who are Bafumbira may join as associate members.

How do I become a member?

To become a member, simply attend any of our weekly meetings held every Friday evening at the MUBS campus, or contact any executive committee member. You will be asked to fill a membership registration form and provide a copy of your student ID. Once registered, you will be added to our communication channels and can start participating in all activities.

Are there membership fees?

No, joining MUBASA is completely free. However, members may voluntarily contribute to specific events or fundraising drives organized by the association. All financial contributions are transparently managed, and members receive receipts for any payments made.

What activities does MUBASA organize?

MUBASA organizes a wide range of activities including cultural celebrations, academic support programs, community outreach trips to Kisoro, sports tournaments, language classes, mentorship sessions, social gatherings, and an annual fundraising dinner. There is something for everyone regardless of your interests.

How can I partner with MUBASA?

We welcome partnerships with organizations, companies, and individuals who share our vision. Partnerships can take the form of sponsorships for events, donations for community projects, mentorship programs, or collaborative initiatives. Please contact the External Affairs Minister or the President through the contact form above to discuss partnership opportunities.

Can non-Bafumbira participate in MUBASA events?

Yes, many of our events such as cultural days, public lectures, and sports tournaments are open to the general public. We believe in sharing our culture and learning from others. However, voting rights and leadership positions are reserved for registered Bafumbira members. Non-Bafumbira students at MUBS can also join as associate members to support our activities.

Still Have Questions?

Our team is always ready to assist you. Do not hesitate to reach out through any of the channels above, or visit our office during working hours. We look forward to welcoming you to the MUBASA family.
